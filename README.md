# Why Does Your Vote Matter?
**Brazilian Presidential Elections 2026: Understanding Voter Behavior Between the First Round and the Runoff**

## Overview
This project analyzes Brazil's 2026 presidential election, comparing the first round and the runoff to understand changes in voter participation, candidate performance, and regional voting patterns.

The analysis also explores results among Brazilian voters abroad, highlighting how electoral behavior varied across different regions and countries.

## Project Objectives
- Compare voting results and voter participation between the first round and the runoff.
- Identify regional differences and changes in candidate vote shares.
- Explore overseas voting patterns and understand what the results reveal about Brazil's electoral landscape.

## Dataset
**Data source:** Tribunal Superior Eleitoral (TSE)

Official dataset: [2026 Election Results – TSE Open Data Portal](https://dadosabertos.tse.jus.br/dataset/resultados-2026)

The project uses two CSV files:
- `detalhe_votacao_munzona_2026_BRASIL.csv` – voter turnout, abstention, valid votes, blank votes, and null votes.
- `votacao_candidato_munzona_2026_BRASIL.csv` – candidate votes by municipality and electoral zone.

## Key Findings
- Lula increased his vote share in all five Brazilian regions and among overseas voters.
- Voter turnout increased in the runoff, while the national abstention rate decreased from 21.08% to 19.22%.
- Lula won the runoff with 51.79% of valid votes, reversing the first-round result.
- Overseas voting revealed significant differences across continents and countries.
- Regional and international results highlight the diversity of Brazil's electoral landscape.
